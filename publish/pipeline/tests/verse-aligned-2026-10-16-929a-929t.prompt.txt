EARLIER ENGLISH DRAFT FOR STYLE ONLY
Hera raged and quarreled with her husband.
Out of this quarrel she bore a glorious son,
Hephaestus, without making love with Zeus, who carries the aegis.
His handiwork surpassed that of all the heavenly gods.
But Zeus, away from Hera, lay with the lovely-cheeked daughter
of Oceanus and Tethys with her beautiful hair.

[Gap in the Greek source]

He deceived Metis, though she knew so many tricks.
He seized her in his hands and put her inside his belly,
afraid she might bear another child stronger than his thunderbolt.
That was why Zeus, son of Cronus, enthroned on high,
living in the upper air, suddenly swallowed her.
At once she conceived Pallas Athena.
Then the father of gods and humans gave birth to Athena
from his head, beside the banks of the river Triton.

Metis, Athena’s mother, stayed hidden
beneath the organs inside Zeus’s body.
She shaped what was right and knew more
than all the gods and mortal humans.
There the goddess had found a place; from there,
her handiwork surpassed that of all the immortals
who have their homes on Olympus.
[She made the aegis, Athena’s armor that sends armies fleeing.]
With it, Zeus brought Athena to birth, wearing her battle gear.

p021 (265–286): Thaumas married Electra, daughter of deep-flowing Oceanus. She gave birth to swift Iris and the lovely-haired Harpies, Aello and Ocypete. On their fast wings they keep pace with gusts of wind and birds, darting high through the air.

Ceto bore Phorcys the fair-cheeked Graiae, gray-haired from birth. That is why the immortal gods and humans who walk the earth call them Graiae, the Old Women: Pemphredo in her beautiful robe and Enyo in her saffron-yellow robe.

Ceto also bore the Gorgons, who live beyond famous Oceanus, at the world's far edge toward Night, where the clear-voiced Hesperides live. They are Sthenno, Euryale, and Medusa, who suffered a terrible fate. Medusa was mortal; the other two could never die or grow old. Dark-haired Poseidon slept with Medusa in a soft meadow among spring flowers.

When Perseus cut off Medusa's head, great Chrysaor and the horse Pegasus sprang out. Pegasus got his name because he was born beside the springs of Oceanus; Chrysaor got his because he held a golden sword in his hands.

Pegasus took flight, leaving the earth, mother of flocks, and reached the immortal gods. He lives in Zeus's halls, carrying thunder and lightning for Zeus, the wise planner.

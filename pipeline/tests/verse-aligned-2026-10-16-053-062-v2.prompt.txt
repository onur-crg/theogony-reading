For nine nights wise Zeus slept with her, climbing into her sacred bed far from the other immortal gods. When a year had passed, the seasons had turned, the months had waned, and many days had run their course, she gave birth to nine daughters, all of one mind, a little way from the highest peak of snowy Olympus. Their hearts are set on song, and their spirits are free from care.
